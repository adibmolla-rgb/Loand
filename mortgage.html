How this estimate works

Monthly repayment uses the standard formula for a repayment mortgage, where each payment covers interest plus some of the loan so it's paid off by the end of the term.

Borrowing capacity multiplies your income by 4.49, the standard limit published by Barclays, Lloyds Banking Group (incomes under £40,000), Nationwide and TSB (incomes up to £50,000).

Purchase tax uses the official bands for Stamp Duty (England and Northern Ireland), LBTT (Scotland) or LTT (Wales), including first-time buyer relief where it exists. It assumes the property will be your only home, so surcharges for additional homes aren't included.

Lenders' limits compare your LTV, income multiple, loan size, term and (if given) age with each lender's published numeric rules, using only rules confirmed by an official source. Special routes, such as family-assisted mortgages, are shown separately and never used to judge your figures.

It doesn't include lender fees, legal costs or insurance, and it assumes the rate stays the same for the whole term.
