Estimated cost over the deal is the interest you would pay during the initial period plus the product fee, for the mortgage amount and term above. It assumes the fee is paid up front, the rate stays fixed for the period, and no overpayments, valuation or legal costs. It is an estimate for comparing deals, not a quote. "Not published" means the lender's sheet doesn't state it; it does not mean none applies. Rates marked "may be out of date" are more than 7 days past the date shown; check the lender's current sheet. Products open only to specific groups are left out unless you tick the box.

LoanD provides estimates and research for guidance only. It is not a lender and cannot approve or reject applications. Lenders make their own decisions using their own criteria, and published criteria can change.
